About RainSentinel

Data-Driven Multi-Hazard Weather Prediction and Decision Support System.

What this system does

  • Ingests live weather observations and forecasts for any Indian district by coordinates.
  • Scores flood, crop-damage, and overall hazard risk from weather and soil signals.
  • Explains each score with feature-level influence, in plain terms.
  • Surfaces early warnings and plain-language advisory guidance for at-risk conditions.
  • Lets you simulate scenarios and compare their hazard outcomes side by side.

Architecture

The frontend, weather/API layer, scoring engine, and data storage are kept as separate modules so a trained ML backend (Random Forest / XGBoost / LightGBM / LSTM) can be swapped in for the current heuristic scoring function without touching the UI. See window.RS_ENGINE in the page source for the integration point.
